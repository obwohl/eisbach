Eisbach water temperature

A 96-hour probabilistic forecast for the Eisbach in Munich, regenerated three times a day. Each plot carries its own issue time.

The model that has been running since the start: 2.7 M parameters, trained on this river, 16 days of context. Bands are q0.01–q0.99, q0.05–q0.95 and q0.25–q0.75.

A zero-shot foundation model, not trained on this river, reading a full year of context and eight covariates. It reports deciles only, so its bands are q0.1–q0.9 and q0.2–q0.8. Those are narrower than DUET's outermost band by construction, not by confidence — q0.1–q0.9 covers 80 % where q0.01–q0.99 covers 98 %. Compare like with like.

With backtests

The same forecast alongside backtests at −96 h, −192 h and −288 h. Solid backtests are honest — they are forecasts that were really made at the time, or replays against the weather forecast as it was then issued. Dashed ones are oracle backtests, computed with the weather that actually occurred, so they flatter the model and are not evidence of real-world skill.
